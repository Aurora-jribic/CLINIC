FAQs

A few helpful answers.

Have a question about a particular service? These answers can help you find the right next step.

Which Aurora service should I choose?

Choose Adult Primary Care for Medicare-based primary care and eligible home visits, DOT Exams for a commercial driver medical certification, or Direct Primary Care for ongoing care through a membership model. Visit each practice’s site for full details.

Do the three services accept the same insurance?

No. Adult Primary Care accepts Original Medicare Part B and select plans as described by that practice. Sarasota DPC is membership-based and does not bill insurance for membership services. DOT exams are self-pay. Please confirm coverage, fees, and eligibility with the specific practice before booking.

Where is Aurora Clinic located?

Our office is at 405 Interstate Blvd Unit C, Sarasota, FL 34240. All visits are by appointment. Some Adult Primary Care services may be provided in a patient’s home when appropriate.

What should I bring to a DOT physical?

Bring your driver’s license, a current medication list, glasses or hearing aids if used, and any relevant medical records. If you use a CPAP, bring your compliance report. The 1111DOT exam page has more details.

Can I use Medicare for Sarasota DPC membership?

No. Sarasota DPC does not accept Medicare as payment for membership services. Patients should keep health insurance for services outside the membership, such as hospital care, specialist visits, imaging, and prescriptions. Medicare-covered primary care is available through Aurora Adult Primary Care.
